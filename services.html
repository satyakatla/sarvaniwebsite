WHAT I DO / 02

Nine ways to
move a deal forward.

Each service can be commissioned individually or combined into a single engagement, scoped around the question your team needs answered.

THE SERVICE MENU
01RISK & SCREENING

Background & due diligence research

Company profile, ownership and management mapping, litigation and adverse-media checks, plus a red-flag memo with risk ratings and next steps.

USED FORPre-LOI screening; confirmatory checks on a counterparty, founder or supplier.
02ORIGINATION

M&A support: screening & market mapping

Agreed screening criteria, longlist-to-shortlist rationale, market maps by subsector and target profiles.

USED FORBuilding a buy-and-build pipeline; testing a new sector or geography.
03VALUATION

Comparable analysis & valuation

Trading comparables, precedent transactions, multiple benchmarking and an indicative valuation range with triangulation.

USED FORPricing an offer; testing seller expectations; investment committee papers.
04PERFORMANCE

Financial & operational analysis

KPI and margin review from filings or management accounts, revenue quality, cohort and unit economics, and peer benchmarking.

USED FORUnderstanding target performance; quality-of-earnings preparation.
05TRANSACTION MATERIALS

Deal materials: CIMs, teasers & pitch books

Anonymised teasers, CIM drafting, management presentations, pitch and credentials decks.

USED FORLaunching a sell-side process; pitching for a mandate.
06COMMERCIAL INSIGHT

Qualitative & quantitative research

Expert and customer interview programmes, surveys, TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing and data analysis.

USED FORValidating an investment thesis; commercial due diligence support.
07SECTOR INTELLIGENCE

Industry & sector reports

Sector primers covering market size, growth drivers, competitive landscape, regulation and deal activity.

USED FOREntering a new sector; investor updates; thought leadership.
08OUTREACH

Buyer & investor identification

Strategic and financial buyer lists, investor longlists with fit rationale and contact routes.

USED FORPreparing a sell-side or fundraising outreach programme.
09ONGOING INTELLIGENCE

Deal & market monitoring

Regular trackers of transactions, funding rounds, management moves and news for your sectors or watchlist.

USED FORKeeping a pipeline warm between deals; portfolio monitoring.
